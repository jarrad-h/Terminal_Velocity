# Pirates — art direction

Pirates have their own design language, separate from Standard Orbital Works: dark faceted armour with oxide-red plates dominating, a hull that steps out in tiers, exposed engines running hot (orange cores, glowing pipework, heat bleeding onto the hull), scratches, chipped edges, rust and crude paint. Weapons are mounted asymmetrically.

Same technique as SOW: painted at high resolution, reduced to 1:1 with a 64-colour palette, light baked from the top-left, art facing up.
